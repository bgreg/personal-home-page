About

Greg is a senior software engineer who has spent his career turning rough ideas into products people love. He comes from a Ruby background and now works mostly in TypeScript.

Outside of work he runs Ruby meetups and leads technical book clubs. He also makes shoes and tends bar, slow crafts where the details are the whole job.

Lately most of his curiosity goes into AI: how it's built, and how its adoption is changing the way we build software. He's put frontier models into production and reshaped his own workflow around agentic tools.
